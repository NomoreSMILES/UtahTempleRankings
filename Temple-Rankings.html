Utah Temple Rankings

Rate each temple from 0–10. The list automatically ranks temples from highest to lowest using the weighted overall score. Your favorite and “Would Visit Again?” choices are personal preferences and never change the main score.

Scoring formula

Every category is scored from 0 to 10. The implementation uses the detailed eight-category formula from your example, which totals 100%.

CategoryWeight
🏛️ Architecture25%
🌳 Grounds20%
✨ Overall Experience20%
📜 Historical Significance10%
⭐ Uniqueness25%

Ratings are saved in this browser. The score values can also be edited directly in the temples array in script.js.
